About Us

MORE THAN A RESTAURANT.

The Pink Cactus is Waukegan’s Mexican-inspired kitchen, cocktail bar, and after-dark lounge — all under one roof.

The

Who We Are

Food, Drinks & Good Energy

We built The Pink Cactus around three things: bold Mexican-inspired flavor, cocktails worth staying for, and a room that feels alive.

By day, it’s aesthetic brunch plates, bottomless mimosas and golden-hour photo ops. By night, the lights go low, the music comes up, and the lounge takes over. Either way, it’s a place built for friends, dates, and celebrations that don’t want to end early.

Every plate is made to share, every drink is made to linger over, and every visit is designed to feel like an occasion — not just dinner.
